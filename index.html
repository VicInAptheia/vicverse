The Structure Stays. The Story Changes.
A tool about lines you can’t undo, and colors you can rewrite.

Lines

You start in Line Mode.
Every black segment you draw is final.
There is no undo, only the next line.

The outer frame was here before you arrived.
Your lines divide the white into rooms,
like choices slicing time into separate days.

You cannot erase a line.
You can only decide where to place the next one.
Every black line is final.
You can't erase it — only add the next one.

Colors

In Color Mode,
only your heavy lines remain.

Inside each region, you are free.
Red, yellow, or blue —
and you may repaint the same region again.

The past shape stays the same,
but its meaning is yours to change.
The lines stay.
But you can repaint the spaces.
